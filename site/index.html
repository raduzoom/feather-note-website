What you get

A native Mac app

Fast to open, light on memory, and at home on macOS: no web view, no account needed to write.

Markdown you own

Every note is plain Markdown. Export your whole library to a folder of .md files any time, for free.

Bring your notes

Import from Evernote and Notion, notebooks, tags, checklists and attachments included. Nothing is dropped.

Sync, end-to-end encrypted

Feather Cloud keeps your Macs in sync and is free during the first release. It stores only encrypted data it can't read.

Or run your own server

The sync server is open source. Run it with Docker on your own machine and point the app at it.

Nothing lost in a conflict

When two devices change a note at once, Feather keeps both. Nothing is overwritten.

Questions

Is Feather Note free?

Yes. Writing, search, import and export are free and need no account. Feather Cloud sync is free during the first release.

Can Feather Cloud read my notes?

No. Notes, titles, tags and attachments are encrypted on your Mac before they're uploaded, and only your devices hold the key. The server stores data it can't decrypt.

Can I import my notes from Evernote or Notion?

Yes. Feather Note imports Evernote exports (.enex) and Notion exports, with notebooks, tags, checklists and attachments.

Which Macs does Feather Note run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Does Feather Note work offline?

Yes. Every note is stored on your Mac, and the app makes no network request until you choose to sync.

Can I run my own sync server?

Yes. The sync server is open source (AGPL-3.0) and runs with Docker. Connect the app to it under Settings › Advanced.
