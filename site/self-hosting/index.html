Self-hosting

Run your own sync server

The Feather Cloud server is open source (AGPL-3.0). It stores only encrypted data, so even on your own machine it can't read your notes.

Read the self-hosting guide

What you need

  • A machine with Docker and Docker Compose
  • A domain name, for HTTPS
  • About 1 GB of memory, and disk space for your notes

Connecting the app

In FeatherMark, open Settings › Advanced, enter your server's address and choose Connect to Server. Trial and plan screens don't appear for your own server.
